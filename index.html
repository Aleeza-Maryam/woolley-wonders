About the Maker

A hook, some yarn,
and a lot of patience.

Woolley Wonders began as evenings spent looping yarn on a small couch — now it's a small studio devoted to slow, handmade craft. Every piece passes through one pair of hands, start to finish, using natural and eco-conscious fibers sourced with care.
